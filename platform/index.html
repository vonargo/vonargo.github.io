COMPUTING RESOURCE FLEXIBILITY

A platform that adapts
to available compute.

The platform connects to model-serving endpoints on the dedicated appliance and on compatible third-party hosts. Routing work between resources is distinct from splitting a single model across devices.

Third-party compatibility is a development objective and depends on hardware and runtime testing. No vendor partnership or certification is implied.
