TRUST AND CONTROL

Know where your AI runs.
Know what it can access.

Local computing creates a practical foundation for organizations that want to govern sensitive information. Private AI is being designed around clear boundaries, verifiable sources, and controlled access.

Security and compliance depend on the final deployment, policies, software configuration, and independent validation. No certification is claimed.
01

Local execution

Models and information services can operate within infrastructure your organization controls. So the most private things can stay on systems you control: a bank feed, a contract draft, a patient record.

02

Scoped access

Users, applications, and information sources need explicit boundaries and accountable actions.

03

Traceable information

Source references and human review help make AI-supported work easier to verify.
