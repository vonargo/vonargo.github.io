DESK-SIDE DESIGN

Designed for
the desk side.

A thin horizontal footprint intended for placement underneath or beside a desk. Planned liquid cooling, power integration, component access, vents, and cabling are all part of the engineering work. Acoustic and thermal behavior remain to be validated.

SCALE REFERENCE: UNDER THE DESKILLUSTRATIVE CONCEPT IMAGE, NOT FINAL HARDWARE
Horizontal and low

About 1.5U to 2U in target height. This is an engineering target, not a final dimension.

Deep enough to cool

Adequate depth for components, airflow, and service access, sized by validation work.

Power envelope

A sub-2,000 W design target, pending validation of the complete system.

HARDWARE STRATEGY

Proven accelerators.
Deliberate engineering.

Our initial configuration builds around reclaimed datacenter accelerators. We validate the interconnect, controller, cooling, and software stack as a complete system.

The broader platform is designed to support different compatible accelerator generations.

Performance figures will be published only when measured on the completed system.

ENGINEERING DETAILS

For the technical buyer.

Proposed configuration and open validation items
  • Proposed: four NVIDIA Tesla V100 SXM2 accelerators with 32 GB HBM2 each, which is 128 GB of HBM2 in aggregate across separate devices.
  • Proposed: one RTX 2000 Ada 16 GB specialist accelerator.
  • Proposed: two 2 TB NVMe storage drives.
  • Validation item: a switched SXM2 carrier and a BlueField-2 BF2500 controller. These are compatibility and topology questions, not a proven standalone GPU-host design.
  • Targets: custom liquid cooling and a power envelope under 2,000 W.
  • Validation item: runtime and model support must be verified on the older accelerator architecture.
  • Benchmarks will be shown only when taken on the completed system.

Separate accelerator memories do not form one coherent model-memory pool. Component names identify proposed parts only. No vendor partnership, endorsement, or tested compatibility is implied.
