Sheet 02 · System

Not a server. Not a GPU. The GPU subsystem.

A conventional GPU server makes you buy a second CPU, a second bank of RAM, a second boot disk, a second everything, just to get four accelerators. PopAI V128 assumes you already own a workstation. It supplies the part you're actually missing: memory, compute, and an NVLink fabric, attached to the machine you already have.

If you genuinely need another server, buy the server. PopAI V128 is for the researcher, lab, or firm that already has the computer and needs the accelerator half of an NVLink machine sitting next to it.

Sheet 04 · Provenance

Reclaimed, not mysterious

Real silicon, real history

Sourced from documented secondary-market and datacenter channels, and individually qualified before installation. Volta is eight-year-old, thoroughly proven compute, not a hobbyist gamble.

Qualified before it ships

Each unit is burned in and validated as a four-way NVLink pool before it leaves the shop, not assembled and shipped untested.

Someone you can call

US-based support and warranty on the finished appliance. The board is a commodity; the qualification, the support, and the accountability are not.

Sheet 05 · Software

A known-good image, not a promise about forever

PopAI V128 ships with a documented, tested software image. We tell you exactly what runs on it, not that everything will, forever. Volta needs `float16`, not bfloat16, and quantized MoE models are where this hardware is genuinely strong.

  • Linux, tested and supported out of the box
  • vLLM, llama.cpp, Ollama, verified working
  • 4-way NVLink pool, tensor-parallel inference
  • MoE and quantized models, the strongest fit
  • No cloud required, workload stays on your network
