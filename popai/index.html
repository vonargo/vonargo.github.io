Why PopAI

Not a server. Not a GPU.
Your AI subsystem.

A conventional GPU server means a second CPU, a second bank of RAM, a second boot disk, a whole machine bought just to get four accelerators. PopAI assumes you already own a workstation and adds only the part you're missing: memory, compute, and an NVLink fabric.

If you need a whole server, buy a server. PopAI is for teams that already have the computer and need the accelerator half of an NVLink machine next to it, for private document and knowledge assistants, high-volume document processing, coding assistance, internal agents, research, and sustained local inference. You bring the workload, PopAI brings the capacity.

Qualified datacenter hardware

Reclaimed, not mysterious

PopAI V128 is built on NVIDIA V100 SXM2 modules reclaimed from decommissioned datacenter systems, not new silicon. Every module is inspected, integrated, and load-tested as a four-way NVLink unit before it ships, with vonArgo support behind the finished appliance.

If your workload genuinely needs newer hardware, we'll tell you plainly when we talk through it.
